A one-page newspaper front page summing up the world news of Tuesday,
24 November 2026.

Lead story: Russian warplanes enter South Korea's air-defence zone near
Ulleung Island, triggering a shelter alert for residents. Also on the
page: Zelensky's call for four-way peace talks, Ukraine defending the
Libya ship sinking as self-defence, Tuesday's stock rally, and
Singapore's continuing Megaspeed probe.

`main.py` copies the bundled `entry.png` (1024x1024) to `/output/entry.png`.
Pure standard library, deterministic — every run writes identical bytes.

All facts on the page were verified against at least two reputable outlets
on 24 November 2026; see NOTES.md for the sources.
